# Festival and artist video outreach

Use these as starting points only after checking the event date, hiring route, named contact, and whether shooting is permitted. Replace every bracketed field; never send the brackets. Do not claim a festival pass, artist relationship, accreditation, or concert footage you do not have. No outreach has been sent from these templates.

## Festival or promoter content-team opening

**Subject:** Video/content team interest for [event or season]

Hi [name],

I’m Farhan, a Vancouver-based filmmaker and videographer. I’m interested in helping [organization] capture [specific event/show] as part of its content team. I noticed [specific detail about the show or their recent coverage], and I’d love to contribute footage or short-form edits that fit that style.

Here are two relevant samples: [sample 1] and [sample 2]. My broader portfolio is [portfolio link]. I shoot on a Sony FX30 and can discuss the lenses, audio setup, deliverables, and turnaround you need.

I work full-time, so evening and weekend shows are the best fit; I can discuss occasional festival dates in advance. Do you have a part-time, seasonal, or event-content application route, or a production partner I should contact?

Thanks for your time,
Farhan Naushad
[phone] | [portfolio]

## Independent artist or manager – a specific show

**Subject:** Video coverage for [artist] at [venue] on [date]

Hi [name],

I’m Farhan, a Vancouver-based filmmaker. I saw that [artist] is playing [venue] on [verified date]. I’d love to discuss filming a short performance or behind-the-scenes piece for your own channels, if that would be useful.

Two samples of my work are [links]. For this show, I could propose [one specific deliverable, e.g. a 30-second vertical recap plus selected clips] with a turnaround of [realistic timing]. I’d confirm the scope, fee, filming permission, artist/venue approvals, and footage usage with you before the show.

Is there someone on the artist or management team who handles video coverage? My portfolio is [link]. I’m generally available for evening and weekend shows.

Thanks,
Farhan Naushad
[phone] | [portfolio]

For major touring artists, contact the artist's management, tour content lead, or approved production company through a published professional channel. Avoid sending the same generic pitch to a festival and every artist on its lineup. A press pass is for editorial coverage and does not itself make you part of the paid content team.
